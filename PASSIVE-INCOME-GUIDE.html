1. Executive Summary — the honest picture

The plan: a solo software developer uses AI agents plus custom code to build a stack of small, recurring-revenue services — starting at ~RM60–200/month in running costs — with a realistic target of RM8,000–12,000/month within 12–18 months, requiring under 10 hours/week maintenance at maturity.

The core thesis: products are cheap for a developer to build — leads are the bottleneck. Therefore: build the lead-generation machine first, then sell three products to the same clients, then let content and data assets compound.

Three truths to internalize before starting:
  • Nothing is truly passive at first. Every idea needs 1–3 months of build and iteration before it becomes low-maintenance. The agent reduces labor; it doesn't eliminate the build phase.
  • Most attempts earn little. Probability-weighted, expect RM50–300/month early. The plan wins by stacking client-based services, not by one lucky idea.
  • "Passive" in 2026 = supervised autonomy. Agents drift and platforms ban automation. The realistic model is 5 minutes/day of human oversight — which is itself a product opportunity.

9. Sales Essentials — Outreach Rules & Pricing Ladder

Non-negotiable sending rules

  • Volume: 20 new emails + 5 video audits per day, maximum
  • Follow-up cap: 3 touches total, then stop
  • Compliance: real name + business address in footer, working unsubscribe, no misleading subjects (CAN-SPAM / GDPR / Malaysia PDPA)
  • Personalization floor: every email references a specific observed problem — no problem found, skip the prospect
  • Scoring: only email 4–5 star prospects (unanswered reviews +2, no chat/booking +2, slow site +1, new business +1, falling 4.0–4.5 rating +1)

The two sequences

SequenceFlow
A — #30 Review responseEmail 1: "You have N unanswered reviews — I'll reply to your last 3 free so you can judge quality" → Email 2 (day 3): attach the drafted replies → Email 3 (day 7): "Should I keep handling these or leave it with you?"
B — #17 Audit (high-value targets)Email 1: name a specific friction ("no online booking; the contact form has no phone field") + offer a free 2-min video → Loom script: their problem (0:15), the cost (0:45), your demo fix (1:30), the 30-day free pilot offer (2:00) → Day 25: results email converting pilot → RM800/mo retainer
