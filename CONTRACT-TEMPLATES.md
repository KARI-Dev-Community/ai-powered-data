# Client Contract Templates

Short-form agreements for pilot and retainer work (#30, #7, #19, #35). Plain-language, solo-operator scale — have a lawyer review before high-value deals. Fill every `{{placeholder}}`.

---

## 1. Pilot Agreement (free 30-day trial)

> **PILOT SERVICE AGREEMENT**
>
> **Between:** {{Your name / business, SSM no.}} ("Provider") and {{Client business, rep name}} ("Client")
> **Effective:** {{date}} · **Term:** 30 days from effective date
>
> **1. Service.** Provider will provide {{service description, e.g., "response to all new Google reviews of Client's business within 24 hours, in Client's voice, with sensitive matters flagged for approval"}}.
>
> **2. Fee.** RM0 during the Pilot. The Pilot converts to a paid Service Agreement only if both parties sign one.
>
> **3. Client obligations.** Client will provide access to {{Google Business Profile / website / calendar}} and respond to approval requests within 24 hours.
>
> **4. Approval.** All responses are drafted by Provider; responses marked sensitive require Client approval before posting.
>
> **5. No guarantee.** Provider does not guarantee specific review scores, ratings, or revenue outcomes.
>
> **6. Confidentiality & data.** Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential. Provider processes Client customer data only to perform the Service, and deletes it within 30 days of termination on written request.
>
> **7. Liability.** Provider's total liability is capped at fees paid (i.e., RM0 during Pilot). Neither party is liable for indirect losses.
>
> **8. Termination.** Either party may end the Pilot at any time with written notice (email is sufficient).
>
> Signed: __________________ (Provider) · __________________ (Client)

## 2. Service Agreement (monthly retainer)

Use after pilot conversion. Key clauses beyond the pilot version:

> **2. Fee.** Client pays **RM{{amount}}/month**, invoiced on the 1st, payable within 7 days via {{FPX/Stripe/transfer}}. Late invoices pause service after 14 days.
>
> **3. Scope & changes.** Scope is {{deliverables}}. Additional scope is quoted separately at RM{{rate}}.
>
> **4. IP.** Client owns final delivered content (review responses, documents). Provider retains its tools, prompts, and workflows.
>
> **5. White-label.** Provider may perform the service under Client's branding; Provider will not disclose its involvement to Client's customers unless required by law.
>
> **6. Term.** Monthly rolling; either party cancels with 30 days' notice.
>
> *(Plus pilot clauses 4–8.)*

## 3. White-Label / Reseller Clause (for #7 agency partners)

Add to an agency's own MSA or use as a side letter:

> Provider supplies the platform/service under Reseller's brand. Reseller sets resale pricing and owns the end-client relationship. Provider never contacts end-clients directly. Reseller is responsible for end-client compliance with platform ToS; Provider warrants reasonable skill and care only. Revenue split: {{% or wholesale price}}. Term: monthly rolling, 30 days' notice.

## 4. One-off / per-lead addendum (#17)

> Leads are "qualified" when they meet the agreed criteria: {{e.g., business owner, within service area, requested a quote}}. Invoiced RM{{X}} per qualified lead, reported weekly with evidence (screenshot/log). Duplicate leads and leads delivered twice are not billable. Minimum monthly commitment: {{RM{{X}}}}; unused leads don't roll over.

## Usage rules

1. **Never start paid work without a signed agreement** — pilot counts (signed = email "OK, let's start" + your written confirmation).
2. Log every signed agreement's date, client, and RM value in `TRACKER.md` → Decision log.
3. Prices in contracts must match your cold quote in `OUTREACH-TEMPLATES.md`; discounts below floor require a deliberate decision logged in the tracker.
