# Legal & Tax Notes — Malaysia

AI-Powered Data & Lead Platform. Turn local market data into warm leads.

Practical compliance notes for a solo Malaysian operator running this data and lead platform as side income. **This is not legal advice — verify current rules with SSM/LHDN or a professional before acting.** Rules below current as of Aug 2026.

---

## 1. Business registration (SSM)

| Stage | Action | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| First RM (validation phase) | Not strictly required to test the waters, but any real invoicing means you should register | — |
| First paying client | Register sole proprietorship (`Pendaftaran Perniagaan`) via SSM's Ezbiz portal | RM30/yr (personal name) or RM60/yr (trade name) |
| If it grows / you want liability separation | Consider Sdn Bhd (private limited) | RM1,000+/yr, needs company secretary |

Do it at your **first invoice**, not before — no point registering before revenue exists.

## 2. Income tax (LHDN)

- Side income is taxable **in full** — employment income (Form BE) + business side income goes on **Form B** (or declare under "other income" if not yet registered as a business).
- Declare from year one, even at a loss — losses can offset future income.
- Keep records: every invoice, subscription receipt (VPS, AI APIs, domain), and the TRACKER.md ledger doubles as your source documentation.
- Tax is on **profit**: your RM60–200/mo tooling costs are deductible business expenses.
- Online income is visible: payment gateways (Stripe/Billplz) and platforms report to LHDN; assume everything is seen.

## 3. SST (service tax)

- Registration required only above the **RM500,000/yr** threshold for most taxable services (some categories differ) — your plan stays far below this; **no action needed until revenue approaches it**.
- Track cumulative revenue in TRACKER.md so you notice at ~RM400k, not RM550k.

## 4. E-invoicing (LHDN)

- Rolling mandatory rollout by revenue band; smaller businesses phase in last (through 2026–2027).
- Practical rule: **below the thresholds, issue normal invoices** (numbered, dated, your SSM registration number once you have one). When LHDN notifies your band, onboard via MyInvois.
- Clients above the threshold may ask for e-invoices from you early — if one does, their onboarding team will guide you; it's quick for a sole prop.

## 5. PDPA (personal data)

You will process third-party personal data (scraped business contacts, client customers):

- Don't resell scraped personal contact lists as a product — use them for the outreach purpose only (also your ToS safety).
- When handling client customer data (#30, #35), sign/issue a simple data-handling clause (in the contract templates) and delete on request.
- 2024–2025 amendments added mandatory breach notification and DPO appointment for qualifying handlers — at solo scale, your duty is: collect minimally, protect access, delete when done.

## 6. What you are NOT (important boundaries)

- **Not selling financial, legal, or employment services.** This platform sells market data, scored leads, and local-business ops tooling.
- **Not reselling personal contact lists.** Scraped contacts are for outreach only.

## Decision checklist

| Event | Action |
|---|---|
| First invoice | Register sole prop with SSM; open separate bank account |
| Monthly, ongoing | Update TRACKER.md ledger; keep receipts |
| Year-end | File Form B with business income + expenses |
| Cumulative revenue nears RM500k | SST registration; consult accountant |
| Any client asks for e-invoice | Onboard via MyInvois |
| Handling client customer data at volume | Re-read PDPA section; consider DPO advice |
