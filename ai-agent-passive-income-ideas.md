## Reading the skills column

Two rough buckets emerge:

- **No-code / prompt-driven** (#1, #4, #12–14, #18, #20, #22, #25, #30): low technical bar, mostly about prompt quality, platform know-how, and consistency.
- **Backend/dev-heavy** (#7, #10, #11, #19, #26): these map onto backend and cloud development skills — full-stack dev, API design, data pipelines, and DB design experience shortens the learning curve considerably.

## How to read the other columns

- **Risk** = capital loss + platform/regulatory exposure, not effort.
- **Capital** = rough starting cash in RM (converted from USD at ~RM4/USD), excluding your own time.
- **Success Probability** = realistic odds of reaching meaningful recurring income (not "any income") within a year, assuming reasonable execution — not guaranteed outcomes.
- **Time to First RM** = to first ringgit or to reasonably hands-off operation, whichever is more relevant to that model.

## Practical notes

- **Lowest risk / fastest starts:** #4, #12, #18, #20, #25, #30 — low capital, low regulatory exposure, short feedback loops.
- **Highest risk, approach with caution:** #5 (trading signals — regulatory/compliance risk in most countries), #28 (legal automation — liability risk), #10 (micro-SaaS — technical + market risk stacked).
- **None of these are truly "passive" at the start** — they're low-maintenance *after* 1–3 months of setup and iteration. Treat "passive income" claims (from courses, YouTube gurus, etc.) with skepticism; the agent reduces ongoing labor, it doesn't eliminate the build phase.
